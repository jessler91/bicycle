        Why cycling helps

        A better bike position can make the benefits easier to enjoy.

        Cycling can be transport, exercise, recovery, exploration, and quiet time. Good fit education helps riders notice comfort signals earlier and ride more consistently.

        +

        Heart and endurance

        Regular riding can support aerobic fitness while letting effort scale from gentle spins to structured training.

        ↕

        Joint-friendly movement

        Pedaling is low impact for many riders, and fit awareness can reduce avoidable knee, hand, neck, and back irritation.

        ◐

        Mental reset

        Rhythm, outdoor light, and route choice can make cycling a practical way to decompress and rebuild focus.

        ⌂

        Everyday mobility

        Bikes can turn errands and commutes into useful movement, often with less cost and less friction than car-only trips.

        Different bikes ask different things from the body

        Road drops

        Often faster and lower. May increase low back, neck, and hand demand when the reach is long or bars are low.

        Road tops

        Usually more open and relaxed than the drops. May reduce back demand while giving up some aero position.

        Commuter

        More upright and easy to scan traffic. May reduce hand pressure but can shift more load onto the saddle.

        Mountain

        Stable and ready for terrain. May feel wider through the shoulders and more dynamic through hips and knees.
